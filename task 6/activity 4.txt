1.Open an Existing Incident:
 Go to Incident → Open and select an Incident where Impact is currently set to High.


2.Change Impact:
 Update the Impact field from High to Medium. This will test the Reverse if false functionality of your UI Policy and the behavior of your Client Scripts.


3.Verify the Field Behavior:


4.Assigned To should no longer be mandatory.


5.Urgency should now be editable, meaning the read-only UI Policy action is reversed.


6.Save the Record:
 Click Submit to save the changes. The record should save successfully without errors, confirming that the reverse condition works as intended.
